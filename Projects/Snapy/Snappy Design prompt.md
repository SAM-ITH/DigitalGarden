#### core system

I want to design an app for students that is based on flash cards. When a student wants to refresh their memory on a specific subject or unit, they can open the app, select the subject and unit, and it will display a set of flash cards related to that topic. They can then review the material. All these flash cards are curated by a group of teachers who specialize in the subject, and they are stored in the database. It will retrieve this data from the backend and show the cards to the students. The app will use the space repetition technique to remind students to revisit the flash cards that they couldn't answer correctly. The splash card has two systems of cards. 

1) The card will show a question and 4 answers like MCQs. When they click on one of the answer cards, the flip shows the correct answer and indicate the student if he answered that question correctly or not.
2) The card will show a question to the student. The student can think of the answer and check if the answer is correct by flipping the card.
